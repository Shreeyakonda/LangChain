HR Policy Document

Company Name: Google
Document Title: Human Resources (HR) Policy
Effective Date: 7/11/2025
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
The purpose of this HR Policy is to define the rules, procedures, and standards that govern employee behavior, workplace conduct, and company operations. The goal is to ensure a fair, respectful, and productive working environment for all employees.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, contract, and temporary employees of the company, regardless of position or department.

3. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to act professionally, respectfully, and ethically at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or any form of misconduct will not be tolerated.
Confidential company information must not be shared outside the organization.
Employees should comply with all company policies, laws, and regulations.

4. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
A lunch break of 1 hour is provided between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM.
Employees are expected to report on time and inform their supervisor of any absences or delays.

5. Leave Policy
Casual Leave: 12 days per year.
Sick Leave: 10 days per year (medical certificate may be required).
Earned Leave: 15 days per year (carry-forward allowed up to 45 days).
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal or via email to the manager at least 3 days in advance (except in emergencies).

6. Attendance and Punctuality
Employees must mark attendance daily through the designated system.
Repeated tardiness or absenteeism may lead to disciplinary action.
Work-from-home options require prior approval from the reporting manager.

7. Employee Benefits
Health insurance coverage as per company policy.
Provident Fund and gratuity contributions where applicable.
Access to employee learning and development programs.
Festival bonuses or incentives at the company’s discretion.

8. Performance Management
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year (mid-year and annual).
Feedback and goal-setting sessions are encouraged for continuous improvement.
Promotions and increments are based on performance, skill, and conduct.

9. Disciplinary Policy
Misconduct, violation of policies, or unethical behavior will be subject to disciplinary action.
Actions may include warnings, suspension, or termination based on severity.
Employees have the right to appeal against disciplinary actions within 7 days.

10. Resignation and Exit
Employees must provide a minimum of 30 days’ notice before resignation.
All company property (ID card, laptop, documents) must be returned.
HR will conduct an exit interview to gather feedback.

11. Equal Opportunity
The company is an equal opportunity employer. All hiring, training, promotion, and compensation decisions are made without regard to race, gender, religion, age, disability, or any other protected status.

12. Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed annually by the HR Department and updated as needed.
Employees will be informed of any significant changes in writing.
